How it works
Three steps to
actual plans.
01
Tell us what you're into
Pick your interests — basketball, hiking, craft beer, chess, live music, whatever — and when you're generally free during the week. Takes about 3 minutes.
02
Get matched with guys nearby
Buddy surfaces men in your area who share your interests and overlap with your schedule. You see a match score — no mystery about why someone showed up.
03
Make a specific plan
Propose something concrete — "pickup hoops Saturday 10am, Wicker Park courts, 2 spots open" — or browse and join plans other guys have posted.
Why Buddy
Built around how
men actually connect.
🎯
Activity-first, not profile-first
You're not browsing faces. You're browsing plans. The who emerges naturally from the what — the same way it works in real life.
📅
Schedule-aware matching
A great match who's never free when you are isn't a match. We factor in availability from the start so suggestions are actually actionable.
🛡️
Built-in trust layer
Phone verified accounts, activity ratings, and a trust score on every profile. You know who you're meeting before you show up.

Groups, not just pairs
Most activities are better with 3–6 people. Buddy is built around group plans, so you're joining a thing — not just meeting one stranger.
